# Safety and Responsible Use

## Authorization

Use Exzera Tool only with devices you own or have explicit permission to inspect or service. Follow all applicable laws, service policies, and data-protection requirements.

## Protect Customer Data

- Obtain the customer's consent before inspecting a device.
- Back up important data before performing maintenance.
- Avoid exposing personal information in screenshots, reports, or support requests.
- Store customer and device records securely.

## Review Before Acting

Security findings may require professional judgment. Review package names and detection reasons carefully before approving removal. Removing a system or trusted application can affect device functionality.

## Keep Operations Stable

- Maintain a stable USB connection during an active operation.
- Keep the computer connected to reliable power.
- Do not disconnect or restart the device unless the application instructs you to do so.
- Stop and investigate unexpected warnings before continuing.

## No Circumvention Guidance

This documentation describes user-facing product functions only. It does not provide procedures for bypassing security controls, defeating access restrictions, or servicing devices without authorization.
